Two-Crew Strategy
This baseline schedule uses two concurrent crews to fit 8 widely-scattered counties and a structural repair into the 66-working-day contract window.
Crew 1 — Joint Cleaning & Sealing (mobile crew). Works Items 438-7004 (CL3) and 438-7007 (CL7) at all 33 locations, sequenced into 8 county-based mobilization trips (A–H), 41 production days total.

Crew 2 — Structural Repair (concrete beam / CFRP specialty crew). Dedicated to Item 788-7001 (Concrete Beam Repair ×3) and 788-7002 (CFRP Repair ×1), all at Location 24 (SL 467 @ IH-10, Sutton Co.). Scheduled Oct 23 – Nov 6 (11 days) to run concurrently with Crew 1's Sutton County trip (G), sharing one TCP mobilization at the shared site instead of two.

Schedule Reserve (Days 44–66)
Nov 16 – Dec 18 · 23 working days held as contingency, not pre-assigned to specific locations.
  • Weather delay days (rain-outs, especially during the Sept–Nov severe weather season in this district)
  • Contractor Force Account "Safety Contingency" work zone enhancements (per General Note, Item 502)
  • Punch-list / rework at any of the 33 locations
  • Final QA walkthroughs, RPLS right-of-way marker replacement documentation (if triggered), and closeout paperwork
  • Demobilization
All 43 production/travel days of active work land well inside the 66-day window, leaving roughly 23 days (~35%) of float — a healthy contingency margin for a multi-county rural contract, consistent with the CPM progress schedule required under Item 8, "Prosecution and Progress."
Key Assumptions
  • Start date: Monday, Sep 14, 2026 (per user input). The plans' 90-day delayed-start provision for sealant/repair-component procurement is assumed already satisfied prior to this date.
  • Contract duration: 66 working days (per user input), ending Friday, Dec 18, 2026. Calendar excludes weekends and observed holidays (Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving + the day after).
  • Joint cleaning & sealing production rate: 300 LF/day combined CL3+CL7 per crew, with a 1-day mobilization minimum per site — a planning assumption, not derived from the plans' PCMS/TMA day quantities (those reflect sign/attenuator-day payment measurement, not literal site-occupation duration; e.g., many dissimilar-quantity urban crossings carry an identical flat 8-day PCMS allocation).
  • Concrete Beam Repair (788-7001) & CFRP Repair (788-7002): assumed ~2 days/repair for the 3 conventional beam repairs (6 days) and ~5 days for the single CFRP repair (surface prep, multi-layer bonding, cure) = 11 days total for the dedicated structural crew.
  • Traffic control: mobile/shoulder-based TCP layouts (TCP(1-2), TCP(1-4), TCP(1-5)) for joint cleaning, and short/intermediate-term stationary layouts (TCP(6-1), TCP(6-3), TCP(6-4)) for the beam repair site, per the Traffic Control Plan General Requirements sheet. No lane closures with barrier or freeway closures are contemplated.
  • EB/WB and NB/SB paired structures (Locs. 18/19, 20/21, 22/23, 25/26) are treated as one mobilization each, worked concurrently.
  • This is a baseline schedule for planning purposes. Per Item 8, "Prosecution and Progress," the Contractor must submit its own sequence of work and CPM progress schedule for approval — this document is a starting point, not a substitute.
